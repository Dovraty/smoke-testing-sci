What is O says Oh!?

A playground where children from age 5 learn the first big ideas behind computers: coding (a computer follows exact steps) and artificial intelligence (a computer learns from examples).

  • Short games of about five minutes. O speaks every instruction, so no reading is needed.
  • Kids learn by trying, making mistakes and fixing them, the way real programmers do.
  • No sign-up and no ads. Stars are saved only on this device.
